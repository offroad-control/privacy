Off-Road Control

Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026. Applies to the Off-Road Control app for Android (com.offroad.control.v1).

In short

  • No account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your trips, locations, vehicle data and settings stay on your tablet. We do not run a server that receives them.
  • To draw the map, the app downloads map images from MapTiler, which sees the map area you are viewing and your IP address.
  • A diagnostic report or an exported route leaves the tablet only when you choose to share or save it.

1. Who we are

Off-Road Control publishes the Off-Road Control app, the companion app for the Off-Road vehicle controller. For any question about this policy or your data, email vcnguyendeptrai@gmail.com.

2. What the app uses, and where it goes

DataWhyWhere it goes
Location (precise and approximate) Show your position on the map, measure speed and heading, record trips and trails, guide you back home. Stays on the tablet. Never sent to us.
Map area and IP address Download map tiles and offline map areas. Sent to MapTiler AG (Switzerland) over HTTPS with each map request. See MapTiler's privacy policy.
Vehicle data Battery voltages, temperatures, tank levels, engine readings, vehicle angle and output states, for the dashboard, alerts and switching outputs. Exchanged only between the tablet and your controller, over Bluetooth or the controller's own WiFi. Stored on the tablet.
Camera video Show live feeds from cameras on your vehicle. Streamed over your vehicle's WiFi and shown live. Not recorded, not sent anywhere.
What you enter Vehicle name, output names, waypoints, camera addresses, preferences and calibration. Stored on the tablet.
Diagnostic reports and route files (GPX, KML) Help with support; take routes to other apps. Created on the tablet. They leave it only when you share or save them, to the app or place you choose.
Controller firmware Update your controller. Comes from the app itself or a file you pick, and goes only to your controller.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data, or share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software, and does not use an advertising ID.
  • The app has no user accounts and sends nothing to servers run by us.

4. Permissions

  • Location: the map, speed, trips and the way back home. On Android 10 and 11 it is also needed to find the controller over Bluetooth.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): find and connect to your controller.
  • Notifications: the notice Android shows while the app keeps working in the background.
  • Network and WiFi: download maps, join the controller's WiFi for faster data and firmware updates, and reach your cameras.
  • Foreground service: keep the link to your controller and trip recording running while the screen is off.

5. Storage, retention and deletion

Your data is kept on the tablet until you delete it in the app (trips, waypoints, cameras) or uninstall the app, which removes all of it. The app opts out of Android backup and device-to-device transfer, so its data is not copied to your Google account or to a new device. We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on a server. MapTiler handles the data in map requests under its own privacy policy.

6. Security

Data on the tablet is protected by Android's app sandbox. Map requests use HTTPS. The link to your controller uses Bluetooth pairing or the controller's password-protected WiFi.

7. Children

The app is a tool for vehicle owners and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's data.

8. Google Play

Google Play handles the download and updates of the app under Google's privacy policy.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app starts to handle data differently, for example by offering an optional online service, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
