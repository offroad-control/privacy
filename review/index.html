Off-Road Control

Foreground service demo

Recorded on the head unit (Android 13) with the app connected to an Off-Road controller over Bluetooth.

  1. 0:00–0:11: TRIP → START TRIP. The trip starts recording.
  2. 0:12–0:23: the app goes to the background. The Recording trip notification of the foreground service (types location and connectedDevice) stays in the notification shade.
  3. 0:24–0:29: back in the app, the trip is still recording.
  4. 0:30–0:41: GAUGES. Live data from the controller kept arriving the whole time.

One unrelated notification of another app is blurred. The same connectedDevice service type keeps the controller link alive during a firmware update (2–4 minutes). Direct link to the file: offroad-fgs-demo.mp4.
